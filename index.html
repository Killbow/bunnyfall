How to play

Three steps, and the first two only happen once.

  1. Get Daggerfall.

    Free from any of these.

    • Steam
    • GOG
    • DaggerfallGameFiles.zip

  2. Press Play and pick your ARENA2 folder.

    On Steam it is under DF/DAGGER/ARENA2. Pick it or drop it on the page; on a phone, hand over the zip. You will not be asked again.

  3. Then play.

Desktop app
The same game in its own window, for Windows and Linux. It updates itself when a new version is out. WindowsWindows portableLinux Windows may warn the first time: More info, then Run anyway.
