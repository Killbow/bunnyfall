-- BUNNYFALL VAMPIRE EXPANSION, PHASE 1 (2026-11-20, the owner's blueprint): THE ELDER'S SUMMONS. Sent, never taken: a
-- vampire clan's letter is one DFU sends unasked (QL-OFFERED's own exception), by courier, a few nights after the
-- turning and to every vampire already standing (systems/vampire/vitae.js elderLetterDue; again a week after a summons
-- left to lapse). The Elder is one of the player's own bloodline (%vam), met BY NIGHT in a tavern of the region; the
-- meeting awakens the Beast (`vamp awaken`: Vitae, the Beast's stages, Blood Sight, feeding in Steal mode), and the rite
-- is three Kisses no one sees (`vamp clouded 3`). The `vamp` lines are systems/vampire/vampireActions.js. The cure ends
-- this quest with the clan's (racialQuests.js endVampireQuests).

--	Quest start-up:
	_letter_ used do _readletter_
	give pc _letter_ notify 1020
	say 1021
	place npc _elder_ at _haven_
	start timer _wait_

_readletter_ task:
	log 1010 step 0

_night_ task:
	vamp night

_clicked_ task:
	clicked npc _elder_

_awake_ task:
	when _clicked_ and _night_
	say 1012
	vamp awaken
	log 1015 step 1
	clear _clicked_

_day_ task:
	when _clicked_ and not _night_ and not _awake_
	say 1013
	clear _clicked_ _day_

_kissed_ task:
	vamp clouded 3

_rite_ task:
	when _awake_ and _kissed_
	say 1017
	log 1018 step 2

_notyet_ task:
	when _awake_ and _clicked_ and not _rite_
	say 1016
	clear _clicked_ _notyet_

_done_ task:
	when _rite_ and _clicked_
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
